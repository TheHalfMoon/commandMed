# commandMed

**Status:** RESEARCH PLANNING — NO TRAINING AUTHORIZED

commandMed is a research program to build a universal, multimodal Health & Medical Intelligence system that can serve patients, caregivers, clinicians, nurses, pharmacists, students, and researchers while targeting exceptional on-device efficiency.

The project optimizes for **verified health and medical intelligence per byte, joule, and second**, subject to hard clinical-safety gates. Benchmark wins, training completion, or model size alone do not constitute success.

## Current state

This repository is intentionally being initialized documentation-first.

Until a bounded Spec Kit specification explicitly authorizes otherwise:

- do not download or train model weights;
- do not run fine-tuning, continued pretraining, distillation, DPO, GRPO, RL, or QAT;
- do not create patient-facing clinical claims;
- do not ingest PHI or restricted datasets;
- do not use external model outputs as training data;
- do not declare a winning backbone;
- do not claim SOTA or benchmark records.

The first implementation target is the evaluation, safety, provenance, and model-tournament foundation required to make later training scientifically defensible.
