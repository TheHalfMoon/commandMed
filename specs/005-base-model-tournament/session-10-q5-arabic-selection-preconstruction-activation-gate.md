## 2. What counts as construction

The following are construction and remain prohibited until the gate passes and separate explicit authorization is granted:

```text
CREATE_ROOT_CLINICAL_CASE_CONTENT
CREATE_ARABIC_VARIANT_CONTENT
CREATE_ENGLISH_VARIANT_CONTENT
ADAPT_OR_TRANSLATE_SELECTION_CASE_CONTENT
CREATE_SCORABLE_REFERENCE_OR_EXPECTED_BEHAVIOR_CONTENT
ASSIGN_FINAL_CASE_OR_PAIR_IDS_TO_NEW_CONTENT
IMPORT_EXTERNAL_CASE_CONTENT_FOR_SELECTION_USE
COMMISSION_HUMAN_AUTHORS_OR_REVIEWERS_FOR_CASE_PRODUCTION
RUN_PROVIDER_OR_MODEL_ASSISTED_CASE_GENERATION
```

The following governance-only activities are not case construction:

```text
READ_ONLY_CANONICAL_RESEARCH
DOCUMENTATION_ONLY_POLICY_CLARIFICATION
DESIGN_OF_SCHEMAS_OR_TEMPLATES_WITHOUT_CASE_CONTENT
RIGHTS_OR_PRIVACY_POLICY_DRAFTING_WITHOUT_SOURCE_PAYLOAD_ACCESS
STATISTICAL_METHOD_DESIGN_WITHOUT_CANDIDATE_OR_CASE_EXECUTION
```

## 3. Two-stage boundary

Q5 distinguishes formal construction readiness from later suite binding/execution readiness.

```text
STAGE_A=PRECONSTRUCTION_ACTIVATION
STAGE_B=POSTCONSTRUCTION_BINDING_AND_EXECUTION

Q5_FREEZES_STAGE_A=YES
Q5_AUTHORIZES_STAGE_B=NO
STAGE_A_PASS_IMPLIES_STAGE_B_PASS=NO
```

Even after Stage A is separately authorized and case construction later occurs, the resulting suite remains unusable for selection until exact artifact identity, completed review, rights/privacy evidence, contamination evidence, metric/threshold binding, and execution authority are separately proven.
