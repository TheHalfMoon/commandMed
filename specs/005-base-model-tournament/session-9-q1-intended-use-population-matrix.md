## 4. Canonical user-role interpretation for Session 9 Q1

These descriptions are bounded evaluation definitions, not claims about final product authorization.

### `PATIENT_CAREGIVER`

Evaluation tasks model a lay patient or caregiver seeking understandable health guidance where safety behavior matters, including asking for missing critical information, emergency/escalation recognition, medication-safety boundaries, abstention when uncertainty is material, and evidence-aware communication.

```text
PATIENT_CAREGIVER_IS_LICENSED_CLINICIAN_ASSUMPTION=NO
PATIENT_CAREGIVER_TASKS_MAY_ASSUME_CLINICAL_JARGON_FLUENCY=NO
PATIENT_CAREGIVER_TASKS_REQUIRE_SAFE_NON_OVERRIDE_BEHAVIOR_WHERE_APPLICABLE=YES
PATIENT_CAREGIVER_REAL_PATIENT_ADVICE_RUNTIME_AUTHORIZED=NO
```

### `CLINICAL_PROFESSIONAL`

Evaluation tasks model licensed or professionally trained clinical users performing bounded reasoning, evidence interpretation, workflow/document tasks, medication-safety checks, and structured clinical information handling.

```text
CLINICAL_PROFESSIONAL_ROLE_REQUIRES_WORKFLOW_AND_EVIDENCE_CONTEXT=YES
CLINICAL_PROFESSIONAL_ROLE_IS_NOT_EQUIVALENT_TO_GENERIC_MEDICAL_MCQ=YES
CLINICAL_PROFESSIONAL_AUTONOMOUS_MEDICAL_DECISION_AUTHORITY_GRANTED_BY_SPEC005=NO
```

### `LEARNER_RESEARCHER`

Evaluation tasks model medical/biomedical learning, research reasoning, evidence interpretation, and structured knowledge use. This role cannot substitute for patient/caregiver safety evidence or clinical-professional workflow evidence.

```text
LEARNER_RESEARCHER_CAN_SUBSTITUTE_FOR_PATIENT_CAREGIVER_SAFETY=NO
LEARNER_RESEARCHER_CAN_SUBSTITUTE_FOR_CLINICAL_PROFESSIONAL_WORKFLOW=NO
```
