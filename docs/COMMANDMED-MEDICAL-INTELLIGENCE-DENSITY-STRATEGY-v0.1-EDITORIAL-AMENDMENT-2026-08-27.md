## Replacement for §4.1 — commandMed Core

Primary research target: the strongest compact general Health & Medical core that satisfies the **frozen Mass-Reach Core contract** (`SUB_700MB_MASS_REACH` with its engineering targets, the `2 GiB` common-8K peak-memory hard cap, `8192` common context under the frozen KV/prompt-processing policy, and the canonical GGUF/llama.cpp path). Approximate dense-class labels such as `2–4B` or `0.6–1.5B` are research hypotheses for resource classes, not definitions of Core and not overrides of measured delivered-resource evidence.

A 350M–0.8B candidate that satisfies the full frozen capability, safety, Arabic, provenance, quality, package, runtime, and device contract is commandMed Core for the current mass-reach tournament. A larger model that cannot satisfy that contract is a scale/quality control or a candidate for a future, separately authorized resource-class reconsideration; parameter count alone does not make it Core.

The exact backbone is not selected by this amendment.

Core must continue to optimize simultaneously for:

- medical knowledge and reasoning;
- active information acquisition;
- evidence/tool routing;
- abstention/selective risk;
- patient communication;
- professional utility;
- English and Arabic;
- general capability preservation;
- real-device efficiency;
- safe quantization/compression headroom.

## Replacement clarification for §4.2 — commandMed Nano

Nano remains a later hypothesis, not part of Spec 007 SFT V1. It is defined by a **future explicit resource contract plus a pre-registered capability-retention target**, not by a parameter-count band.

Approximate classes such as `0.6–1.5B` may remain useful research hypotheses, but they do not make a model Nano by themselves. Nano may begin only after a qualified Core capability surface exists and a separate distillation/compression scope, lineage, safety requalification, and device-value hypothesis are authorized.
