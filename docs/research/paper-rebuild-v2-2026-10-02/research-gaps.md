# Research gaps and rejection rules

G1: Jointly quantify same-backbone readout differences and decision-versus-maintenance adaptation effects on proper scoring and held-out autoregressive competence. This is a conditional gap after a bounded search, not a firstness claim. Visual Jev provides a matched head control; Dyad explicitly addresses capability preservation; Uni-Med and Med-MoE-LoRA study interference. Our matrix must be rejected as a novelty claim if a source already reports the same design and endpoints [@arxiv260925845; @arxiv260936116; @arxiv240917508; @arxiv260107935].

G2: Ask whether a calibration map frozen on clean evidence transports across verified missing, conflicting and outdated source conditions. MemSafe and Visual Jev already combine source interventions and selective prediction; RULE and SURE-RAG already supply calibration and risk controls. The secondary paper needs validated intervention semantics and stronger transport evidence, not renamed sufficiency labels [@arxiv260932269; @arxiv260925845; @arxiv240705131; @arxiv260503534].

G3: Remove a duplicated encoder while preserving its conditioning contract and a measured quality/resource frontier. Width projection is a necessary interface operation, not a scientific solution. Existing unified models and PixelUMM directly threaten architectural novelty [@arxiv260938597; @arxiv250514683].

Rejected as generic novelty: unified medical understanding/generation; a typed probability head by itself; evidence-aware abstention by itself; Arabic support by itself; applying a known merge algorithm to medical donors. These may be engineering components or controls, but need additional causal or empirical evidence to become paper claims.
