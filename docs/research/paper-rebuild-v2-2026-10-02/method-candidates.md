# Method candidates and readout controls

Baseline order is frozen LM candidate logits, frozen-backbone head/probe, matched adapted LM logits, matched jointly trained typed head, structured answer generation including invalid outputs, and a separately served decision specialist if admitted. Restricted-logit and option-slot readouts are mechanisms, not donor performance certificates. Kev pointer, decider answer-slot and CLM state/action projections were statically inspected in V1; their source/model pins remain in prior-input-identities.json. Kev's 5120-width pointer can be dimension-compatible with the 27B text path while decider-2B (2048) and CLM (4096) cannot be transplanted as-is. Equal widths do not establish representation or tokenizer alignment.

The proposed head is an option-scoring pointer: project the final question marker and each option-closing marker to a common latent dimension, take scaled inner products and softmax across the supplied options. It is trained afresh on the chosen base, never copied from a dimensionally different donor. Compare a simple linear head and an equal-parameter MLP control; validation chooses head dimension and regularization within the same budget. An arbitrary-candidate interface and a fixed-label classifier are separate ablations, because candidate parsing and token length can change the task.

Do not require image generation to answer this question. Do not add an optimizer or merging method unless a measured failure motivates it. Readout performance is compared both on the original trained head and on identically fitted post-hoc probes of each frozen adapted backbone. This reduces the risk of confusing representation changes with extra head supervision. A same-backbone comparison is still not a complete causal proof of architecture because readout capacity and fitting are interventions; report them explicitly.
