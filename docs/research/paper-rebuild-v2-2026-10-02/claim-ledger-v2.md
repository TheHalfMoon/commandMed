## Promotion levels

HYPOTHESIS -> DESIGN -> DEVELOPMENT_RESULT -> CONFIRMATORY_RESULT -> REPLICATED_RESULT -> PAPER_CLAIM.

No DESIGN artifact may be cited as an empirical result. “Revolutionary,” “first,” “best,” and “SOTA” are goals/marketing terms, not scientific conclusions unless a narrowly defined evidence ledger explicitly supports them.
