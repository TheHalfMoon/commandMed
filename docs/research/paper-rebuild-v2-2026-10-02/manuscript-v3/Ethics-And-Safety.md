# 9. Ethics and Safety

CommandMed Contract is a research program, not a clinical device or autonomous medical decision maker. The current study explicitly forbids claims of clinical safety or effectiveness.

No protected health information or restricted clinical records are authorized. Dataset admission requires source-level rights review, privacy assessment and exact content identity. Public availability is not treated as permission to train, redistribute or publish derivatives.

A central ethical concern is false confidence. Structured probabilities can appear more authoritative than free-form text even when they are wrong. For that reason, the study evaluates proper scores, selective risk, missing/contradictory evidence and interface stability rather than presenting a type-safe schema as a safety guarantee.

Evidence-changing interventions are used only when their intended semantics can be justified. Artificially deleting information from a case does not automatically create a clinically valid abstention label. Ambiguous interventions must be excluded or adjudicated before model-output inspection.

No generated image or synthetic text becomes evidence about a real patient. No result from this paper authorizes diagnosis, treatment, triage or medication decisions without appropriate clinical validation and governance.
