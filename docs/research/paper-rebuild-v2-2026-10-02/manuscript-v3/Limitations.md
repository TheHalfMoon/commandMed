# 8. Limitations

1. **Construct validity.** Candidate-based decisions and exam-style tasks do not reproduce full clinical decision making.
2. **Transformation validity.** Natural-language paraphrases and evidence edits can unintentionally change semantics; only validated transformations may enter confirmatory analysis.
3. **Pretraining contamination.** Public benchmark exposure cannot be fully excluded for pretrained models; residual risk must be reported.
4. **Calibration transport.** A calibration map fitted on one distribution has no automatic guarantee under another distribution.
5. **Backbone scope.** Two-family replication is stronger than one-family evidence but still does not establish a universal architectural law.
6. **Language scope.** English primary evidence does not transfer automatically to Arabic or other languages; language-specific calibration requires separate validation.
7. **Clinical scope.** No patient data, clinical deployment, prospective trial, or clinical-outcome endpoint is included in the current design.
8. **Resource scope.** Zero-cost constraints may limit model scale; methodological conclusions must therefore be stated at the evaluated scale.
9. **Metric scope.** CED is a proposed diagnostic. Its practical threshold must be justified rather than assumed.
10. **Concurrent work.** Typed-decision, permutation-robustness and medical reliability research is moving rapidly; novelty must be refreshed before submission.
