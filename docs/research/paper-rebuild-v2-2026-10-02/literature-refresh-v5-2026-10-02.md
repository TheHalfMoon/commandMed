# V5 literature refresh — 2026-10-02

Purpose: adversarially test whether the V5 thesis is already substantially occupied. This is a bounded search, not a systematic-review completeness claim.

## Directly relevant prior art

- MedHELM, Nature Medicine 2026, DOI `10.1038/s41591-025-04151-2`: broad multidimensional clinical-task evaluation; kills generic multidimensional-benchmark novelty.
- Wang et al., EMNLP 2025, DOI `10.18653/v1/2025.emnlp-main.1398`: trustworthiness survey spanning factuality, robustness, fairness, safety, explainability and calibration; motivates but does not establish V5 novelty.
- Xia et al., arXiv `2601.08064`: calibration, robustness, semantic stability and sensitivity can disagree; directly threatens broad cross-property claims.
- Matta et al., arXiv `2607.19367`: calibration can improve without structural probabilistic coherence; supports separate assurance axes.
- Liu et al., arXiv `2410.02205`: logical consistency is a distinct reliability target and can itself be improved by intervention.
- Wu et al., arXiv `2604.03216`: confidence quality should be evaluated at the decision-policy level, not only by calibration.
- MedQAbstain, ACL 2026, DOI `10.18653/v1/2026.acl-long.1365`, and Presacan et al., npj Digital Medicine 2026, DOI `10.1038/s41746-026-02882-1`: medical abstention is established prior art.
- ProMedical, ACL 2026, DOI `10.18653/v1/2026.acl-long.1714`: multi-criteria medical alignment and safety/proficiency separation are established.
- AgentMD, Nature Communications 2025, DOI `10.1038/s41467-025-64430-x`, plus Kara and Gunel 2025 and Roeschl et al. 2026: deterministic clinical calculators/pipelines are established and should be treated as oracle/control infrastructure.

## Surviving gap

No source found in this bounded refresh matched the full design: multiple targeted reliability interventions applied to matched medical decision systems, full cross-property paired effects, preregistered interaction terms, noncompensatory assurance decisions, exact medical rule oracles, and independent-family replication. This remains a conditional gap, not a `first` claim.
