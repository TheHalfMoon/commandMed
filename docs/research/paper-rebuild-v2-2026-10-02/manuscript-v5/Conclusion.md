# 11. Conclusion

Reliability in medical foundation-model decisions should not be treated as a scalar property unless the consequences of compensation are explicitly justified. The proposed study asks a narrower and more actionable question: when a reliability defect is repaired, what changes elsewhere?

By combining formal non-implication analysis, paired intervention effects, selected factorial interactions, non-compensatory assurance gates and exact medical-rule oracles, V5 is designed to distinguish beneficial transfer, modularity and interference rather than assuming any of them.

The conclusion of the final paper will be determined by the frozen confirmatory evidence. If material interference is not replicated, the paper will report that negative result and narrow its claims rather than reinterpret the protocol after the fact.
