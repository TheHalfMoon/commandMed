# 5. Experimental Design

## 5.1 Phases

Phase A validates transformation/oracle code on synthetic fixtures with no model claim. Phase B develops interventions on a small permissive open-weight backbone and development data. Phase C executes the frozen confirmatory matrix once under quarantined test access. Phase D replicates only nominated decisive effects on a second independently structured backbone family. Any generalization claim is restricted to the named evaluated model families and task distributions.

A 27B model is not required for the methodological claim. Scaling occurs only when it answers a prespecified unresolved question and lawful zero-cost compute is available.

## 5.2 Task families

The preferred substrate separates three families: `RULE_ORACLE` tasks built from deterministic clinical calculators or executable rule logic; `OPEN_CLINICAL_REASONING` tasks with auditable public labels or qualified adjudication; and separate capability-retention tasks. Rule-oracle and open-reasoning effects are never averaged into one primary endpoint. Each family declares which assurance axes are defined; undefined axes are `NOT_APPLICABLE`.

## 5.3 Primary endpoints

Primary endpoints are the prespecified target effect for each intervention, hard-gate non-target effects, selected ordered `Gamma[a|b,j]` interactions (and `Kappa` order gaps where both orders are meaningful), and the compensatory-versus-noncompensatory decision comparison. Calibration, coherence, stability, responsiveness, action validity and selective behavior retain their native metrics rather than being normalized into one headline score.

## 5.4 Fairness of comparison

Post-hoc interventions share base predictions and calibration data where possible. Learned interventions receive matched training examples, optimizer budget, checkpoint-selection access, parameter-budget reporting and a prespecified set of independent training seeds. Inference calls, latency, peak memory, storage and deterministic-tool calls are recorded. No intervention receives confirmatory feedback unavailable to another method in its comparison family.

Probability-transforming interventions are evaluated under a frozen downstream action/abstention policy as the primary operational contrast. A separately labeled refit-policy contrast may be reported. Endpoint pairs are labeled as structurally distinct, shared-input or mathematically coupled before results, so tautological coupling cannot be presented as novel interference.

## 5.5 Statistics

The source case is the inferential cluster. Use paired effects, cluster-preserving resampling or exact tests as justified, prespecified meaningful margins, explicit multiplicity families and equivalence procedures. Minimum detectable effects and power calculations are frozen before confirmatory access.

## 5.6 Negative controls

Required controls include unchanged-base repeated runs, sham meaning-preserving transformations, calibration-only transformations expected not to alter argmax ordering, no-tool versus deterministic-tool conditions, and matched-capacity/readout controls.
