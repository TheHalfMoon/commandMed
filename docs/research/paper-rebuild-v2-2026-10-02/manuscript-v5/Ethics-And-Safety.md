# 9. Ethics and Safety

This work is an evaluation-methods study, not a clinical deployment study. No PHI, private clinical records, gated clinical datasets or patient-facing experimentation are authorized by the current protocol.

Synthetic or public cases may be used only when their rights and provenance are explicitly admitted. Synthetic generation does not create clinical truth; every target relation used for a primary endpoint requires an external rule, source label or qualified adjudication pathway.

The study avoids optimizing a single aggregate “trustworthiness” score that could hide a safety-critical failure. Noncompensable axes remain visible and can independently block a positive conclusion.

Model outputs must not be used to make real patient-care decisions during development or evaluation. Deterministic clinical calculators are used only as research oracles under their documented scope and cannot be generalized beyond their validated indications.

If later human evaluation is required, the project must establish the appropriate ethics, consent, privacy and compensation pathway before data collection. Automated judging cannot substitute for qualified human evaluation of patient-facing harm claims.
